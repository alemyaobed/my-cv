## Why Germany fits

- Public universities in most states charge no tuition to non-EU
  students. Only a semester contribution of roughly EUR 100-400.
- Exceptions to avoid: Baden-Wuerttemberg universities (Stuttgart,
  Heidelberg, KIT, Tuebingen, Freiburg, Konstanz, Ulm, Mannheim) charge
  non-EU students EUR 1,500 per semester; TU Munich charges roughly
  EUR 4,000-6,000 per semester to non-EU students. LMU and most other
  Bavarian universities remain free (VERIFY current stance).
- Ghana does not need an APS certificate (that is China, India,
  Vietnam only).
- A First Class BSc maps well to German admission bars, and KNUST being
  English-medium may satisfy the language requirement at universities
  that accept a medium-of-instruction letter instead of IELTS/TOEFL
  (VERIFY per university; saves the test fee).

## Full-ride scholarship layer

- DAAD study scholarships: EUR 992 per month plus travel, insurance,
  often rent subsidy. At zero tuition this is a full ride. Lines for
  2027 intake generally close October-December 2026: CHECK THE DAAD
  DATABASE THIS WEEK.
- DAAD EPOS (development-related postgraduate courses): full funding,
  requires at least 2 years of work experience (he has 3). Course list
  is development-leaning; check for data/CS-adjacent courses.
  Deadlines per course, mostly Oct-Dec 2026 for 2027.
- Erasmus Mundus joint masters (EMJM): full scholarships around EUR
  1,400 per month, applications free, deadlines mostly Jan-Mar 2027 for
  September 2027. Language and Communication Technologies (LCT) is a
  strong fit for the language-tutor AI background and includes German
  partners (VERIFY current funding line). Search the EMJM catalogue for
  data science programmes too.
- Deutschlandstipendium: EUR 300 per month, merit-based, applied for
  AFTER enrollment at the university. Treat as a top-up, not a plan.
- Fallback if no scholarship: blocked account of about EUR 11,904 per
  year for the visa. That is the real cost floor of studying in
  Germany without a ride.
