# University of Augsburg, MSc Data Science, required documents

From the official program page, verified 2026-11-19.

## Checklist for applicants with a foreign degree

| Document | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bachelor certificate + Transcript of Records | have | SCHOOL DOCS folder |
| Certified copies / sworn translations | exempt | only for documents not in German or English |
| APS certificate | exempt | China, India, Vietnam only |
| CV | have | polished versions in cv/ |
| English B2 | have | degree from English-speaking institution is an accepted route; EF SET C1 and KNUST letter as backup |
| Module descriptions or similar | to assemble | learning objectives and content for the seven aptitude areas |
| GRE | not required | not mentioned anywhere |
| Motivation letter | not required | not on the checklist |

## Module descriptions, what is needed

Content descriptions (objectives and content, not just titles) for
courses covering the seven areas:

1. Multidimensional analysis, 5 CP: MATH 161/162, pending the
   admissions email on whether this passes
2. Abstract linear algebra, 5 CP: CSM 273, covered by the Asante letter
3. High-level programming, 4 CP: C++, Java
4. Practical programming, 4 CP: C++, Java, Visual Basic
5. Algorithms or numerical methods, 5 CP: CSM 258 covered by the
   Asante letter, Data Structures I and II
6. Discrete structures, databases or data engineering, 5 CP: Discrete
   Mathematics I and II, Databases I and II
7. Data science or machine learning, 4 CP: Introduction to Artificial
   Intelligence
